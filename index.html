On lighting

“Lighting is always the first telltale sign that thought has been put into the environment and the space. In my opinion, lighting can either make or break a space.”

Brea Hunter

That's where every project starts for me: how the room is lit at nine in the morning and nine at night. Then the walls, the materials, and the objects that make it yours.

Study 01

The Living Room

Symmetry first: two sash windows, a black-painted fireplace and a mirror that ties them together. Antique pieces and greenery layered in, so the room reads collected rather than bought in a weekend.

  • Mirror centred on the mantel
  • Matched seating, mixed side tables
  • Plants for height at the windows

The Primary Bath

The Kitchen

The Bedroom

What I do

Services

One room or the whole house, a finishing layer or a full concept. Each service can stand on its own or be combined into one project.

01

Interior design

Room and whole-home design: layout, finishes, lighting plans and furniture, all drawn up and agreed before anything is bought.

02

Decor & styling

For rooms with good bones that need finishing. Art, textiles, lamps and objects, edited and placed so the room feels lived-in and pulled together.

03

Sourcing

Lighting is my favourite thing to source. Fixtures, furniture and finishes found to suit the space, the budget and the look, with everything tracked in one list.

04

Creative direction

Concepts, mood boards and visual direction for brands, shoots, launches and spaces that need a clear point of view.

How we work together

The approach

  1. 01

    Conversation

    We talk through how you live, what you love and what isn't working. Bring photos, Pinterest boards and the things you can't part with.

  2. 02

    Concept

    A mood board, a palette and sketches of the room, so you can see where it's going before a single thing is ordered.

  3. 03

    Sourcing

    Lighting, furniture, finishes and the small details, chosen and organised into one clear plan.

  4. 04

    Styling

    Everything goes in and gets placed, and the room gets its finishing layer. Then you get to walk in.

Design notes

A few opinions I share on Instagram and stand behind in every room.

Walls & mirrors

Change the walls, change the room

“Something as simple as switching up the wall treatments can make the biggest impact on the way your space feels. Mirrors add more light to any space and play with the eyes to make the space feel double the size.”

Details

Small things, strong impact

“I love the small details that create such a strong impact to the overall feel.” A good handle, a lit niche, the right shade on a lamp: these are what people notice without knowing why.

See the post (opens Instagram)

Bathrooms

The shower niche

“Will forever promote a good shower niche.” Plan it with the tile, light it from above, and the bottles stop being clutter and start being part of the design.

Style

Traditional never goes out of style

Mouldings, panelling, brass and arched mirrors have lasted for a reason. I like to bring them in with a lighter hand, so a room feels timeless rather than old.
